## Symbols, footprints, and datasheets

Pin names, pad geometry, datasheet URLs, and package drawings were transcribed
from the manufacturers' published datasheets and KiCad library conventions.
Manufacturer names and part numbers remain the trademarks of their owners.

## Follow-up

- Replace unverified embedded payloads with provenance-documented assets, or
  remove them, before a release decision.
- Keep developer-local absolute paths out of manifests and generated output.
